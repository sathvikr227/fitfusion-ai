  3. Why not 4 weeks from the day file? It leaks the label
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Day file: every injury row follows a gap of >= 22 days (no rows in the 3 weeks before);
  98.9% of other rows follow the previous day. Any history beyond a row's own 7 days
  would tell the model 'this row has a hole before it' = the label. The week file has the 3 weeks
  inside each row, so no history is rebuilt. Leakage screen (no single feature may reach 0.75):
  most separating feature: strength_w0 at 0.584 -> passed
