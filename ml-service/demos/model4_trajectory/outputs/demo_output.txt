  1. Why Model 4 was rebuilt
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Old model: valakhorasani/gym-members-exercise-dataset (pseudo-sequences), accuracy 0.459.
  Its 'sequences' were rows regrouped from a one-off survey, with a proxy completion rate
  and a made-up weekday. It never saw a real person's sessions in order.
  FitFusion's own history is seeded/test data (owner, 2026-10-28), so it is not used.
  New model: real Strava logs, protocol fixed before training (PROTOCOL.md, commit a2de032).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  2. The data and the label
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  9,259,153 run-days from 36,412 runners (2019-2020), 9,186,630 after cleaning.
  1,091,424 windows of 8 consecutive sessions within 56 days.
  Label: median 10 km-equivalent pace of sessions 5-8 vs 1-4. IMPROVE if more than 2.2% faster, REGRESS if more than 2.1% slower, else PLATEAU
  (thresholds = tertiles of the training athletes only).
