  3. The bug that sank rounds 1-2 (computed now, development seeds 400000-400199)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Average reward per week under always-MAINTAIN: -0.376 (fitness drifts down, injuries happen)
  Week 26 was 'truncated', so PPO valued a user who stays as if they trained forever:
    -0.376 / (1 - 0.99) = -37.6   vs   quitting (churn) = -20 once
  -> pushing the user until they quit looked BETTER to PPO. Every round-1/2 seed learned exactly that.
  Round 3 makes week 26 the real end (what the evaluation scores) and the collapse disappears.
